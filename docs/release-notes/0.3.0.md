**Your Android phone, on your Windows PC.** Mirror and control the screen, browse the phone's files and drag them in and out, share one clipboard, and get your phone's notifications and incoming calls on the PC. This is the first release for testers.

## Install

1. Download **TandemSetup.exe** below and open it.
2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because Tandem isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Tandem installs in a few seconds (no admin needed) and opens. Its setup guide walks you through connecting your phone, with steps for your phone's brand.

Tandem updates itself: new versions download in the background and finish the next time Tandem restarts.

## You need

- A Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) or Windows 11 PC, 64-bit Intel or AMD.
- An Android 11 or newer phone on the same Wi-Fi as the PC.

## Good to know

- If Windows says **Smart App Control blocked** Tandem, there's no "Run anyway". You can turn Smart App Control off in Windows Security → App & browser control → Smart App Control, install Tandem, and turn it back on.
- Calls ring on the PC with Answer and Decline. You still talk on the phone.
- Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones need a few extra switches; the setup guide shows where they are.
- Tandem talks to your phone directly over your Wi-Fi. It has no account and no servers; the only thing it fetches from the internet is its own updates, from this GitHub page.
- To uninstall: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Tandem → Uninstall.
